# Devlog 2026-11-27

## Speech voice licence review

- **Question.** Can a free MIT app use or ship the Piper Lessac voices? Finding 0002 had flagged this as unresolved.
- **Surprise.** The Blizzard 2013 Lessac dataset is licensed for research only: no commercial use, no redistribution, licence granted to a named person. Piper's own licensing page says the same and takes no position on whether trained weights are derivatives. Several other popular voices (hfc_male, kusal, libritts_r) are fine-tuned from lessac and inherit the problem.
- **Second surprise.** The "MIT" Piper binary is not clean either: it links espeak-ng (GPL-3.0), and the maintained successor is GPL-3.0 and ships Python wheels, not an exe. A separate process keeps our code MIT; downloading on first run keeps GPL binaries out of our installer.
- **Outcome.** Proposed default is `en_US-ljspeech-medium` (public domain, from scratch, 63.5 MB) on the pinned 2023.11.14-2 binary, both downloaded on first run. Written up in [research 0002](../research/0002-speech-voice-licences.md) with a dated amendment to [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-speech-stack.md).
- **Not done.** No audio was generated: ljspeech on the old binary, its speed and its sound are unverified. Not legal advice.
